# DocumentScanner

I was thinking about creating an assistant like a financial consultant. Not everyone enjoys reading legal and financial terms in contracts. With an assistant, we can take a photo of a contract page and ask a question. And with AI, we'll return a clear answer about the content. For example, "According to the contract, how much extra will I pay for the goods if I take out a loan?" And in response, I'll immediately receive a specific amount without having to delve into all the details of the contract. This saves time and helps us understand the contract details as if we had a personal financial consultant. It helps us identify any gaps or details that interest us.

Input: a photo of the contract and the user's query.
Output: an AI-powered response that explains and saves time.
